## How it works

- A timer moves the buses every second. In your app, take positions from your server instead, for example over a WebSocket or by polling.
- `animate` on [`<Marker>`](/docs/components/marker#moving-markers) slides each marker to its new position. The slide lasts a little less than the update interval (900 ms for updates every second), so the movement looks continuous and one slide ends before the next starts.
- `rotate` turns the icon to the heading, worked out from the direction of travel. Changing it doesn't re-create the marker.
- Each trail is a [`<Polyline>`](/docs/components/polyline) of the bus's recent positions.
- Following a bus calls `setCenter` from [`useMapControls`](/docs/hooks/use-map-controls) after each update.

<Callout>
  `animate` works with HTML and React icons. Keep the marker's other props, such as `title` and `icon`, stable while it moves, since changing them re-creates the marker.
</Callout>
